# A first project in Folio

## Compare options and deliver a brief / 比较方案并交付简报

### English

1. Open the workspace and choose **Compare**. Start with your own decision or **Explore a support software comparison**. The sample opens as a separate fictional project.
2. Name at least two options, edit the criteria and create the comparison. Under an option, choose its existing sources or **Import for this option**. Plain text, Markdown and text-based PDFs work locally.
3. Open the suggested entry and find evidence. If the source uses another language or vocabulary, enter one phrase per line under **Search terms**. Inspect the original passage before recording a fact or marking a finding reviewed. Record material gaps as **To confirm** and explain their impact under **Limitations & open questions**.
4. Write your recommendation and choose **Build decision brief**. If a source changes, review the affected finding, then compare the linked section with your writing before applying an update.
5. In **Delivery**, prepare the recipient preview, save an edition and export HTML. For an editable copy of the entire project, use **Export → Project backup** and save the downloaded file. Restore it from **My workspace**.

### 简体中文

1. 进入工作区并选择「比较」。可以输入自己的决策，也可先打开单独保存的客服软件选型虚构示例。
2. 输入至少两个方案，选择比较维度，创建比较。在相应方案下选择已有资料，或点击「为此方案导入资料」。
3. 点击工作区提示打开具体比较项。在对应方案中检索，遇到不同措辞或语言，可把多个原文词组分行填写。阅读原文，保存结论，再手动核对。资料没有交代的内容可记为「待确认信息」，并写明对决策的影响。
4. 写下建议，然后「写入研究简报」。资料更新后，先核对相关发现，再对照已有正文与新结论，确认要应用的改动。
5. 到「交付」预览读者看到的内容，保存固定版本并导出 HTML。需要带走整个可编辑项目时，选择「导出 → 项目备份」并保存文件，在「我的工作空间」里恢复。

## Review a whole comparison / 连续核对

Link sources to their options and select **Find evidence for all**. Each criterion can have up to three search phrases, one per line. Under **Source updated**, **Not reviewed** or **To confirm**, choose **Start review**. Inspect each passage and its conditions before checking the review box and saving. Use **Brief connections** to review changes to a working brief. A saved delivery keeps its original wording.

在各方案关联资料后点击「批量找证据」。每个维度可设置最多三行检索词。筛选「来源更新」「未核对」或「待确认」，再点击「连续核对」。逐条查看原文与适用条件，确认后勾选并保存。已有简报可通过关联预览更新，已保存的交付版保留原文。

## Research notebook / 研究笔记

To investigate a question outside a comparison, open **Research**, enter your question and add sources. In **Ask Folio**, select your sources and use **Find passages**. Open **Evidence ledger** to inspect the saved passage, page and revision. Keep a finding or add a cited passage to the brief. The optional AI answer mode requires a compatible WebGPU device and a model download.

如果要研究一个开放问题，在「研究」输入问题并添加资料。在「问 Folio」选择资料范围，通过「查找原文」检索。在「证据清单」检查原文、页码及版本，再保留发现或把带引用的段落加入简报。可选的 AI 回答需要支持 WebGPU 的设备和模型下载。

If startup protection appears, **Open workspace safely** loads the workspace on request. **Export saved projects** provides a lightweight backup route. Small backups up to 2 MiB can also be copied as text and restored from **My workspace**.

若出现启动保护，点击「安全打开」进入工作区，或从此页直接导出已存项目。对于 2 MiB 以内的备份，也可以复制备份文本，再从「我的工作空间」恢复。
